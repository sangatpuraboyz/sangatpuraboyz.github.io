About Us

Sangatpura Boyz Entertainment

Sangatpura Boyz Entertainment is the official digital platform dedicated to preserving and promoting the culture, traditions, Gurbani programs, village events and memorable moments of Sangatpura Dhaipai, Jagraon, Ludhiana.


Our mission is to connect people with their roots by sharing high-quality videos, photographs, live streams and local stories.

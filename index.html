ABOUT US

Sangatpura Boyz Entertainment

Punjabi Culture • Village Memories • Entertainment

Sangatpura Boyz Entertainment is the official digital platform of Village Sangatpura Dhaipai, Tehsil Jagraon, District Ludhiana.

Our mission is to preserve Punjabi culture, Gurbani programs, village history, sports, melas, weddings, documentaries and unforgettable memories.

Our Mission

To digitally preserve the history, culture and traditions of our village for future generations.
